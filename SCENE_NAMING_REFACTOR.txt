## Naming Convention Breakdown

### Format: `scene_[action]_[character]_[location]_[level]`

**[action]** - 23 different actions:
- Grabs: wrist, shoulder, neck, ass, waist, thighs, shoulderneck, waistass
- Physical: spank, sniff, sniffbutt, drop, jump, trip
- Interactions: intro, casual, confrontation, firstmeet, gentletouch, sitfeet, historydomination, namemistake

**[character]** - Currently `roman`, scalable for:
- `mayank`
- `aman`
- `nicks`
- `abhi`
- Any future character

**[location]** - 5 locations:
- `hallway` (8 scenes)
- `waterbottle` (6 scenes)
- `watercooler` (6 scenes)
- `messhall` (6 scenes)
- `room` (7 scenes)

**[level]** - 3 progression levels:
- `level1`: Initial contact, first meetings, low intensity (12 scenes)
- `level2`: Escalation, increased physical contact (11 scenes)
- `level3`: High intensity, extreme actions (10 scenes)
