## 🔑 The Naming Convention

### Format
```
scene_[action]_[character]_[location]_[level]
```

### Example Breakdown
```
scene_grabwrist_roman_hallway_level1
      ↓       ↓      ↓        ↓
    action character location level
```

### Components

**[action]** - What happens
- grab_wrist, grab_shoulder, grab_neck, grab_ass, grab_waist
- grab_thighs, grab_shoulderneck, grab_waistass
- spank, sniff, sniffbutt, drop, jump, trip
- intro, casual, confrontation, firstmeet, gentletouch
- sitfeet, historydomination, namemistake

**[character]** - Who's involved
- `roman` (currently all 38 scenes)
- `mayank` (ready for future)
- `aman`, `nicks`, `abhi`, others (structure supports)

**[location]** - Where it happens
- `hallway` - 8 scenes
- `waterbottle` - 6 scenes
- `watercooler` - 6 scenes
- `messhall` - 6 scenes
- `room` - 7 scenes
- Character-specific (2 close-ups)

**[level]** - Intensity level
- `level1` - Initial, safe, low-intensity
- `level2` - Escalation, medium-intensity
- `level3` - Extreme, high-intensity
